Privacy Policy

Your privacy and the protection of information entrusted to Alaric Health Services are important to us.

Last Updated: September 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Alaric Health Services ("Alaric Health Services," "Alaric," "we," "us," or "our") is a U.S.-based healthcare revenue cycle management and medical billing services company.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, protect, and retain information when you visit or interact with our website and related online services.

By using this website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may collect information such as:

  • Name and professional contact information.
  • Email address and telephone number when voluntarily provided.
  • Company, practice, or organization information.
  • Information contained in inquiries submitted through our contact forms.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when requesting information about our services.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device type, IP address, and website usage information where applicable.

We collect information primarily when you voluntarily submit it through our website or communicate with us.

3. Information Submitted Through Contact Forms

Our website may provide forms through which prospective clients, healthcare organizations, business partners, or other visitors can contact Alaric Health Services.

Important: Please do not submit medical records, Social Security numbers, insurance identification numbers, diagnoses, treatment information, or other sensitive patient information through a general public website contact form unless specifically instructed to do so through an approved secure communication channel.

General contact forms are intended primarily for business inquiries, service requests, and communications with our team.

4. Protected Health Information (PHI) and HIPAA

Alaric Health Services provides medical billing and revenue cycle management services that may involve handling protected health information ("PHI") on behalf of healthcare clients where applicable.

Where Alaric Health Services is acting as a business associate under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act ("HIPAA"), we maintain appropriate policies, procedures, and safeguards designed to protect PHI in accordance with applicable HIPAA requirements and our contractual obligations.

Privacy & Security Commitment: Alaric Health Services takes the confidentiality, integrity, and security of information entrusted to us seriously and applies appropriate administrative, technical, and physical safeguards as required by applicable law and our business obligations.

Our HIPAA-related responsibilities may vary depending on the services provided, the role we perform for a client, and the applicable agreement between Alaric Health Services and that client.

5. How We Use Information

Information collected through our website may be used to:

  • Respond to inquiries and requests for information.
  • Communicate with prospective and existing clients.
  • Provide information about our medical billing and RCM services.
  • Evaluate and respond to business opportunities.
  • Maintain and improve our website and online services.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, spam, and unauthorized activity.
  • Maintain website security.
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

6. Disclosure of Information

We do not sell personal information submitted through our website for monetary consideration.

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide requested services or respond to communications.
  • Use service providers that support our website or business operations.
  • Protect the security and integrity of our systems.
  • Comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal processes, or lawful requests.
  • Protect the rights, safety, and property of Alaric Health Services, our clients, or others.

Where HIPAA applies, disclosures of PHI are handled in accordance with applicable HIPAA requirements, contractual obligations, and other applicable laws.
